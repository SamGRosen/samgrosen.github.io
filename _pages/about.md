Originally from Maryland, I came to [UMass Amherst](https://www.umass.edu/) to study Mathematics and Computer Science. Afterwards, I joined the [statistics department at Duke](https://stat.duke.edu/) where I recently completed my PhD, advised by [Jason Xu](https://jasonxu90.github.io/). I have been fortunate enough to pursue opportunities at [General Dynamics Mission Systems](https://gdmissionsystems.com/), [Johns Hopkins University: Applied Physics Lab](https://www.jhuapl.edu/), [DraftKings](https://www.draftkings.com/), and [Datadog](https://www.datadoghq.com). After undergrad and during my PhD, I was a research and development intern at [Genentech](https://www.gene.com) working on the [Epiviz](https://epiviz.github.io/) project and a machine learning intern at [Cruise](https://www.getcruise.com) and [Pinterest](https://www.pinterest.com).

During my PhD, I did research on the intersection of convex optimization and statistics. In particular, I studied theoretical properties of convex clustering, including the effect of [affinity hyperparameters on solution quality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24673), and expanding convex clustering to [sparse biclusterable data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03936). In addition, I examined how techniques from convex optimization could be used to sample from [posteriors restrcited to constrained spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04237).
